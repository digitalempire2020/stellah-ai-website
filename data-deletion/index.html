Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: 24/06/2026

Operated by: Growthfinity Pte. Ltd. (UEN: 202127639G)

Platform Operating Name: Stellah AI

1. Overview

This page explains how to request deletion of personal data associated with Stellah AI.

Stellah AI is used by businesses to manage customer conversations, appointments, and connected messaging channels. Depending on your relationship with Stellah AI, we may act as a service provider to the business you contacted, or as the direct operator of your business account.

2. If You Are a Business Client

If your business uses Stellah AI and you want to delete your account data, connected channel data, or business records, email us at:

Email: support@stellah.ai

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your business name.
  • The email address associated with your Stellah AI account.
  • The connected channels or data you want deleted, if applicable.
  • A contact email so we can verify and process the request.

3. If You Contacted a Business Using Stellah AI

If you sent a message to a business that uses Stellah AI, that business is usually the Data Controller for your conversation data.

Please contact the business directly first and ask them to delete your conversation or customer record.

If you are unsure which business processed your data, email us at support@stellah.ai and include enough information for us to identify the relevant business and conversation.

4. Meta, Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Data

If Stellah AI receives data from Meta platforms such as Facebook Messenger, Instagram, or WhatsApp through a business integration, deletion requests can be sent to support@stellah.ai.

We will verify the request, identify the relevant business account or connected Page, and delete or anonymize data we control unless we must retain it for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or compliance reasons.

5. Processing Timeline

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within a reasonable period after receiving enough information to verify the request.

Some deleted data may remain in backups or logs for a limited retention period before being removed through normal backup rotation.

6. Contact

For data deletion, privacy, or account-removal requests:

Growthfinity Pte. Ltd.

Email: support@stellah.ai
